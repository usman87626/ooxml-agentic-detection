# Data Availability Statement

The implementation, experiment scripts, development/test split, per-sample predictions and a dataset manifest (SHA-256, label, format, source, malware family and VirusTotal verdict for all 324 documents) are publicly available at [GitHub URL] (archived at [Zenodo DOI]). Malicious samples are not redistributed; they can be retrieved from MalwareBazaar by SHA-256 using the provided download script. Benign samples are identified by SHA-256 and source; the complete corpus is available from the corresponding author on reasonable request for research purposes.
